# Hook pattern library

These are structures, not finished lines. Fill each blank with a verified Mario-specific fact, tension, or opinion.

## Confession

- “I wasted [real period] believing [old belief].”
- “The part of starting over I was embarrassed to admit was [truth].”
- “I thought I had found [positive state]. I had actually found [uncomfortable contrast].”

## Personal receipt

- “When [specific event] happened, the hardest part was not [obvious thing].”
- “I knew I had become [unwanted identity] when [observable behavior].”
- “I kept saying [stated standard], but my behavior kept proving [real standard].”

## Contrarian opinion

- “[Common advice] can be terrible advice when [condition].”
- “You probably do not need more [popular solution]. You need [behavioral truth].”
- “People call [behavior] peace. Sometimes it is just [harder interpretation].”

## Identity

- “There is a difference between ‘[event] happened’ and ‘I am [identity].’”
- “You do not miss [past]. You miss [what it represented].”
- “The next version of you cannot keep protecting [old pattern].”

## Standards

- “Your standards are not what you say. They are [observable proof].”
- “Every time you negotiate with [boundary/promise], you teach yourself [consequence].”
- “You keep calling it [soft label]. Your behavior calls it [hard label].”

## Story

- “On [specific day/moment], I almost chose [expected action]. Then [turn].”
- “I remember the exact moment I realized [truth].”
- “This looked like an ordinary [moment]. It changed how I think about [theme].”

## POV / realization

- “POV: you finally realize [precise identity shift].”
- “Maybe you are not [self-judgment]. Maybe you are [more useful interpretation].”
- “Being [current age/state] still beats [future regret].”

## Hook selection test

Choose the hook that best satisfies all four:

1. It creates tension without lying.
2. It sounds natural when Mario says it aloud.
3. It points toward the actual body.
4. It contains specificity, contradiction, or emotional precision.
