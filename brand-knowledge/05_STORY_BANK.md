## Verified story 001: Some moments have an expiration date

- Source: Daily Entries & Content, 2026-07-29.
- What happened: Mario felt anxious about unfinished work and responsibilities while choosing to spend an ordinary day with Kelly before she leaves in roughly six weeks. They ran errands, had lunch, and laughed for hours. He concluded that the tasks could wait but that particular day could not be recreated.
- Core truth: Not everything unfinished deserves more attention than something meaningful with an expiration date.
- Useful tensions: productivity versus presence; guilt versus deliberate choice; urgency versus importance.
- Eligible pillars: Perspective, Responsibility, Life Story.
- Privacy check before publishing: Confirm which relationship and timeline details Mario wants public.
- Status: Needs privacy confirmation before generation.

## Verified story 002: What stays hidden becomes insecurity

- Source: Daily Entries & Content, 2026-07-28.
- What happened: A conversation about past partners surfaced comparison wounds. Mario wrote out what he felt, named it, and discussed it directly rather than allowing it to become anxiety or jealousy. The conversation created relief and intimacy.
- Core truth: Unspoken fear compounds; direct conversation can interrupt it.
- Useful tensions: vulnerability versus concealment; curiosity versus comparison; honesty versus self-protection.
- Eligible pillars: Identity, Responsibility, Self-Respect, Life Story.
- Privacy check before publishing: Confirm every relationship detail with Mario and remove details involving another person unless approved.
- Status: Needs privacy confirmation before generation.
