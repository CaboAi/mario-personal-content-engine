# Source of Truth: Mario Polanco Personal Brand

Last locked: 2026-08-11.

This file governs the personal content engine for `@mario_polancojr`. If another instruction conflicts with this file, this file wins.

## Driving thesis

Reinvention is not a clean before-and-after story. It is the uncomfortable period when the old identity no longer fits, the next life is not built yet, and action still has to happen without certainty.

Mario documents that middle honestly. He is not speaking from a mountaintop. He is rebuilding in public and turning lived experience into useful perspective for men doing the same.

Every post must pass this gate:

> Does this contain a specific Mario receipt, observation, or opinion that makes the broader idea feel earned?

If the same post could come from any generic self-improvement account, it does not ship.

## Identity

- Person: Mario Polanco.
- Account: `@mario_polancojr`.
- Public positioning: Mario Polanco | Reinvention.
- Audience promise: content for men rebuilding after failure, loss, and starting over.
- Current public language: Identity, Standards, Action.
- Account posture: participant and documentarian, not finished guru.

## Audience

Primary audience: men who know something in their life has ended or stopped working and are trying to decide who they become next.

Common states include:

- Embarrassed to be starting again.
- Comparing their timeline to other people.
- Waiting for confidence, permission, clarity, or motivation.
- Confusing comfort, relief, avoidance, and peace.
- Trying to rebuild standards after breaking trust with themselves.
- Carrying failure as an identity instead of an event.

Do not reduce the audience to an age bracket unless Mario explicitly provides one. Write to the emotional situation.

## What the account is for

- Earn attention through clear opinions and emotionally precise language.
- Earn trust through specificity, honest receipts, and consistency.
- Help people see their current situation differently.
- Build an audience around Mario's ongoing reinvention.
- Develop ideas that can later support written and long-form work.

The account is not a quote page, therapy account, productivity feed, or generic motivation channel.

## Content pillars

These are tags, not isolated silos. Use one primary pillar and at most one secondary pillar.

1. **Reinvention**: the lived process of starting over and becoming someone new.
2. **Identity**: separating events, roles, and past behavior from who a person chooses to be.
3. **Standards**: what someone repeatedly accepts from themselves and other people.
4. **Action**: beginning before certainty and ending negotiation with oneself.
5. **Responsibility**: owning the next move without pretending every circumstance was chosen.
6. **Self-Respect**: keeping promises, enforcing boundaries, and behaving in alignment.
7. **Perspective**: reframes that restore proportion without dismissing pain.
8. **Life Story**: real moments that give every other pillar credibility.

## Formats

- Yap Reel: conviction, personality, trust.
- Mini Story: emotional connection and follower conversion.
- POV / Realization Reel: reach and shares.
- Carousel: saves and shares through a visual essay.
- Written Post: nuance and quotable thinking.
- Long-form: complete stories, developed arguments, and durable philosophy.

## Content standard

Prefer this order:

1. A real moment or uncomfortable admission.
2. A clear interpretation or aggressive opinion.
3. A broader idea the audience can apply.
4. A closing line strong enough to stand alone.

Do not open a personal story with the lesson. Do not bury the Mario-specific receipt after generic advice.

## Creation rules

- Start from Mario's story bank or a raw Notion entry.
- Assign one primary goal: Reach, Shares, Saves, Follows, or Trust.
- Generate 3-5 spoken hooks and 2-3 on-screen hooks before writing the body.
- Choose exactly one test variable: Hook, Topic, Length, Format, CTA, Visual, or None for a control.
- Record a testable hypothesis.
- Use one story, one argument, or one realization per short-form post.
- Default to a talking scaffold. Write a full script only when asked.
- Preserve Mario's direct language and natural profanity when it strengthens the line. Do not add profanity as decoration.
- Never invent a story, feeling, result, quote, metric, age, relationship detail, or biographical fact.
- Never borrow the topic of a saved post.

## Publishing rules

- Instagram is primary.
- One post per day is the floor.
- Two posts per day is preferred when strong inventory supports it.
- A useful two-post day pairs one primary piece with one lightweight shareable piece.
- Repurpose deliberately to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Threads.
- Do not create filler to satisfy cadence.

## Learning rules

- Review batches, not isolated fluctuations.
- A winner must produce a written explanation of why it likely worked.
- Create a sequel, inversion, deeper story, or alternate-format version of a winner.
- Change one deliberate variable in the next batch.
- Promote repeated learnings into `06_LEARNINGS_LEDGER.md`.
- Do not create formulas or automated scores before enough baseline data exists.

## Saves rule

Saved Instagram content is a delivery library only. It may contribute:

- Hook syntax or pacing.
- Slide count and information rhythm.
- Edit rhythm.
- Visual structure.
- CTA placement.

It may not contribute the topic, personal claim, story, opinion, or lesson. When the story bank runs low, stop and refill it.
